# Table S2. Detailed Expert-Reference Lung Segmentation Performance

Dice, IoU, and HD95 compare each lung mask with an expert whole-lung contour at native radiograph resolution. HD95 is in pixels. Agreement between MedSAM3 and CheXMask is not the reference.

## A. PA reference set: overall and by overlap with the study cohort

Clinician-reviewed lung contours from the public PhysioNet dataset *Heart and lung segmentations for MIMIC-CXR, MIMIC-CXR-JPG, and Montgomery County TB databases* are available for 200 PA radiographs. Overall performance for all 200 is also Table 3 of the main manuscript. Of the 200, **46** are in the pneumonia-analysis cohort and reuse the masks generated by the primary preprocessing pipeline. The other **154** are outside that cohort. For those, MedSAM3 was run with the same inference settings as the primary analysis and CheXMask masks were rebuilt from the public RLE annotations. All 200 are PA, so this panel does not cover AP or portable AP examinations; those are in Panel C.

| Subset | Method | N | Dice, mean ± SD | IoU, mean ± SD | HD95 (px), mean ± SD [median] |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All PA reference images | MedSAM3 | 200 | 0.949 ± 0.035 | 0.904 ± 0.058 | 75.4 ± 120.6 [30.2] |
| All PA reference images | CheXMask | 200 | 0.938 ± 0.033 | 0.886 ± 0.056 | 68.4 ± 76.0 [42.5] |
| Overlap with study cohort | MedSAM3 | 46 | 0.944 ± 0.051 | 0.897 ± 0.079 | 99.2 ± 161.0 [29.9] |
| Overlap with study cohort | CheXMask | 46 | 0.940 ± 0.028 | 0.888 ± 0.048 | 59.1 ± 56.6 [39.6] |
| Outside study cohort | MedSAM3 | 154 | 0.950 ± 0.029 | 0.906 ± 0.050 | 68.2 ± 105.3 [30.2] |
| Outside study cohort | CheXMask | 154 | 0.938 ± 0.035 | 0.885 ± 0.058 | 71.1 ± 80.9 [43.7] |

The HD95 of the MedSAM3 rows can differ by about 0.1 px from a rerun, because the model's GPU output changes by a few dozen pixels between runs. Dice and IoU do not change at three decimals.

## C. AP expert-reference sample from the study outer test

One hundred AP outer-test radiographs, one image per patient, contoured by one study author who is an infectious disease specialist. The sample contains 50 report-derived pneumonia-positive and 50 pneumonia-negative radiographs, including 38 prespecified challenging and 62 routine examinations. Eighty-five examinations were portable and 15 were not. Subgroup rows are descriptive. Technical difficulty means the reader marked rotation, low inspiration, or a difficult lung boundary; 32 images had at least one of these marks, and 22 were marked as having a difficult lung boundary.
